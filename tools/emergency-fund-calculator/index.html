Emergency Fund Calculator

An emergency fund is not a round number someone else picked. It is your own bills, multiplied. Put in what the month actually costs to keep the lights on and the car running, and this works out one, three and six months of runway, plus how long it takes to get there at the rate you are saving. Nothing is sent anywhere: the math runs in your browser.

No interest is counted here, on purpose. At these amounts and over these horizons a savings-account return moves the answer by less than a month, and a calculator that quietly assumes a return is telling you something you cannot check. The build times are the amount still needed divided by what you save each month, rounded up. Same arithmetic as how much emergency fund you actually need.
